One of the most important common application of XOR is Hamming weight, if you have 2 signals A and B, you need to find the switching between 2 signals. If A is 1010, B is 0101. Here all the transistors are switched. How do you know how many bits have flipped, do XOR. Then you countones, get 4 and know it is 4 switch. This is primary XOR application.
